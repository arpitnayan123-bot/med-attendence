Guide

From zero to clarity in one minute

AttendMate Pro is designed around how a clinical day actually feels: no time for data entry, just enough time for two taps. Here is the full workflow, end to end.

Sign in locally

Open the dashboard and sign in Google-style with your Gmail, name and roll number. Nothing is sent anywhere — the profile lives in your browser's localStorage and instantly resolves your posting sub-group.

Mark the day

Today's view lists every scheduled class from the master timetable. Tap Present, Absent or Cancel per class — or use "Mark all present" and fix exceptions. Sundays are pre-marked as the weekly holiday.

Read the advice

Each subject card shows a live tier — Don't Miss, Be Careful or Safe to Miss — with exact safe-skip or must-attend counts. Check it before deciding to skip.

The everyday toolkit, in detail

Day navigation and catch-up. The arrows around the date header move through any day; "Copy yesterday" clones the previous day's marks, and the bulk-fill card marks a whole past date range as present or absent in one action, skipping Sundays and already-logged days. Started the app late in the session? Back-fill three weeks in under a minute.

Extra and make-up classes. Cancelled a class? Mark it Cancelled so it stops counting against you. Had an unplanned extra class? Add it from the extra-class form — it logs under the right subject and mode and appears in all totals immediately.

Baselines for prior records. If you tracked attendance elsewhere before AttendMate Pro, open any subject's details, enter prior present/total counts for theory and clinical as a baseline, and the app merges them with everything you log from then on.

The what-if simulator. Every subject card includes a simulator: press "+1 attended" or "+1 missed" and watch the percentage and safe-skip math update instantly — without touching your real log. It answers "what happens to Surgery if I skip tomorrow?" before you commit.

Clinical slot credits. The 10 AM – 1 PM posting slot automatically credits the department your sub-group is rotating through on that date, per the official roster. If reality differs, the swap button on the slot re-credits it manually.

Backups and recovery

Everything is stored in localStorage — private, but tied to one browser profile. Settings → Data has you covered: Export backup downloads a JSON of your entire state; Import backup restores it on any device or after clearing browser data; Reset wipes cleanly (also undoable within the session via the 40-step undo stack). Export after any intense logging week — thirty seconds of habit for full peace of mind.

Undo everything: the arrow button in the top bar (with a step counter) appears whenever there is something to undo — wrong "all present", deleted extra class, bad bulk fill — one tap reverses it.
