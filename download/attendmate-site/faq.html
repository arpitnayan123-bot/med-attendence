FAQ

Straight answers

The questions batchmates actually ask before trusting an app with their attendance record. If yours isn't here, the guide page covers the day-to-day workflow in depth.

General
Is this the official NSMCH app?
No. AttendMate Pro is an unofficial, student-built study tool. It replicates and upgrades the AttendMate concept using the published Phase-III (Part-2) timetable, assessment calendar and posting rosters. Always confirm critical dates with the college.
What does it actually calculate?
Per-subject attendance percentages for theory (75% target) and clinical (80% target) modes, exact safe-skip counts, must-attend recovery plans, the percentage cost of the next miss, day and month summaries, streaks, and a session-end projection to the Sentup exam at your current attendance rate.
Can I change the 75% / 80% targets?
Yes — Settings → Attendance targets accepts any value between 1 and 100 for each mode. All advice, tiers and projections recompute against your custom targets immediately.
Does it cost anything?
No. It is a free, offline static website with no ads and no accounts. Download it, open it, use it.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes — the whole site is responsive, from the landing pages to the dashboard. On phones a tab strip replaces the sidebar. It works fully offline once saved on the device.
Privacy & data
Does the Google sign-in send my data to Google?
No. The sign-in is a local simulation: your email, name and roll number are stored only in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is transmitted anywhere — the site makes zero network calls.
Where does my attendance data live?
Entirely in your browser (localStorage), with a JSON export option in Settings. Clearing browser data erases it, so export a backup regularly. Moving to a new device or browser? Import the JSON there.
Can I use it on two devices?
Yes, with a manual sync: export a backup JSON from device one and import it on device two. There is no server, so there is no automatic sync — by design, for privacy.
What happens if I clear my browser data?
Local profiles, logs, baselines and module progress are erased. If you exported a backup, import it and everything returns. If not, the 40-step undo can't help across a wipe — prevention is the export button.
Roster & accuracy
What if a posting or timetable entry looks wrong for my group?
The rosters are entered from the official schedule, but errors can happen and dates can shift. Verify with your class coordinator, and use the swap button on any clinical slot to credit the correct department for that day.
How does my roll number decide anything?
Rolls 1–16 are Group A, 17–32 Group B, 33–49 C, 50–66 D, 67–83 E, 84–100 F, with four sub-groups each (A1–F4). The sub-group determines which allied and specialty departments your minor and mid rotations assign; the parent group drives the major postings.
Are holidays and exams accounted for?
Sundays are the weekly holiday and stop counting automatically. The official internal assessments, the Sentup exam and the exam-oriented clinical classes appear on the calendar and in the analytics horizon.
