Roster

Your roll number is the key

The Phase-III (Part-2) batch of 100 is split into six groups (A–F) and 24 sub-groups (A1–F4). Each sub-group rotates through Allied, Specialty and Major postings on official dates — type your roll number and see your own calendar.

Roll-number lookup

Resolves instantly on this page — no sign-in needed, nothing stored. In the dashboard, the same resolution drives your posting timeline and which department your clinical slot credits each day.

How credit works

Who goes where, and when

Minor rotations cycle four sub-groups at a time through the allied specialties; mid rotations move two sub-groups through the specialty departments; major postings take whole groups into Medicine, Surgery and Obs-Gynae. During the 10 AM – 1 PM posting slot, the department on duty for your sub-group receives the attendance credit.
