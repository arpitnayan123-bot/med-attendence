About

Built by a student, for the batch

AttendMate Pro began as a replica of the AttendMate tool used in Phase-III (Part-2) at NSMCH Bihta — and grew into a ground-up rebuild with real analytics, because "75% required" tells you nothing about today's decision.

Why it exists

Attendance in a clinical phase is genuinely hard to reason about. Postings move your whole sub-group every fortnight, theory runs at 75% while clinical runs at 80%, extra and cancelled classes bend the denominators, and the only tool most students have is mental arithmetic at 8:55 AM. AttendMate Pro takes the official schedule — the master Monday–Saturday timetable, the A1–F4 posting rosters, the assessment calendar — and keeps applying it to your running numbers, so every skip or save is an informed choice rather than a gamble.

The principles

Offline-first. The whole site is static files. Host it anywhere, save it to a laptop, open it in a hostel with no wifi — nothing breaks, because nothing phones home.

Private by architecture. No accounts, no servers, no analytics. Your email, roll number and every mark live in your browser's localStorage; the JSON export exists so you — not a vendor — own the copy that matters.

Grounded in official data. The rosters and exam windows in this site are transcriptions of the Phase-III (Part-2) schedule, not guesses. When the college revises dates, the data is meant to be corrected rather than worked around.

Honest arithmetic. Advice states its assumptions: projections say "if your current rate holds"; simulators touch preview numbers only, never your log; cancelled classes stop counting instead of quietly punishing you.

Disclaimer

AttendMate Pro is an unofficial tool by a student, for students. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced on behalf of Nalanda Medical College & Hospital (NSMCH), Patna, or Aryabhatta Knowledge University. Attendance rules used here (75% theory / 80% clinical) reflect the published Phase-III (Part-2) requirements as understood at build time; the college's word always wins. Screenshots on this site were captured with demo data and contain no real student's record.

Contribute: found a roster error, or the timetable changed? The fix belongs in the data — every date in the roster tables is stored in plain arrays in js/site.js and in the dashboard itself, deliberately easy for any batchmate to correct.
